KTX2 variants were encoded from these originals with Khronos KTX-Software 4.4.2
(ETC1S color, UASTC normal, complete mip chains). scripts/build-surfaces.mjs
is the reproducible conversion command. JPEGs remain as compatibility fallbacks.
