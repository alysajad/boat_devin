# Changelog

All notable changes to Devin Hacks during the 24–25 October 2026 hackathon are recorded here.

## 25 October 2026

- Added server-authoritative left/right rowing with yaw, lateral drift, heading recovery and damped boat roll.
- Added Rapier collision bodies for racing boats, riverbanks and moored houseboats, including continuous collision detection and recoverable impacts.
- Added assigned-side controller instructions, signed balance feedback and automatic solo assistance after a disconnect.
- Calculated finishes from the rotated bow and preserved millisecond tie handling.
- Reworked the rower rig so the top hand grips the paddle handle and the lower hand grips the shaft without stretching arm bones.
- Added external hull roll, limited first-person horizon roll and reduced-motion handling.
- Upgraded the room protocol to version 4 and isolated old sessions.
- Added physics, grip, controller, protocol and first-person browser tests.
- Completed a 15-minute run with 50 controllers, 10 boats and a rendered broadcast.
- Replaced the project documentation with the completed hackathon submission set.

## 24 October 2026

- Started Devin Hacks as a multiplayer Kerala boat-racing experience.
- Built the phone join, motion calibration, swipe fallback and readiness flow.
- Added configurable crews, race formats, force and motion sensitivity.
- Added the server-authoritative race loop, synchronization scoring and secure room capabilities.
- Built the Chundan Vallam-inspired 3D boats, Kerala river scenery and animated rowers.
- Added the laptop race director, camera previews, first-person rower view, fullscreen controls and synchronized crew audio.
